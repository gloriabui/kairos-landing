Frequently asked questions

  • How do I edit my posting after submitting?

    After posting your trip, you'll receive an email with a secure edit link. Use this link to update your posting details, pricing, or cancel the trip anytime.

  • How do payments work?

    Kairos connects car owners and drivers, but payment arrangements are made directly between both parties. We provide guidelines on fair pricing and secure payment methods.

  • What about insurance?

    Both drivers and car owners should ensure they have appropriate insurance coverage. We provide comprehensive guidelines and pre-trip checklists to help verify coverage before any trip begins.

  • How much should I pay or charge?

    Pricing depends on distance, vehicle type, and timeline. We provide suggestions based on similar routes to help you set fair compensation.

  • How are drivers vetted?

    We provide a community rating system and "open to verification" badges for drivers willing to undergo additional checks. Our guidelines also help car owners conduct their own verification steps.
